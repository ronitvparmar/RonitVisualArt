ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Ronit, the creative mind behind Ronit Visual Art — an automotive photography studio based in India.

I specialize in capturing cars with a cinematic and artistic approach, focusing on the design, performance, and personality that make each vehicle unique. Through creative composition, thoughtful lighting, and detailed editing, I turn every project into a compelling visual story — not just another catalog shot.

I work directly with car enthusiasts, dealerships, brands, and private owners across India, creating high-quality automotive photography tailored to every vision.
